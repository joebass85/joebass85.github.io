Giovanna Moralishvili - Producer/AD

About Giovanna

Giovanna Moralishvili (24) is a producer and assistant director for Hiking The Tide. Giovanna started her journey into producing during her time at Emerson College, successfully producing several BFA Thesis short films, one of which being a finalist at the Panorama Trans and Queer Film Festival (Give Me Eden). Post-graduation, she has begun to focus on her skills as an assistant director while continuing to work as a freelance producer. Hiking The Tide is the most ambitious film Giovanna has worked on in her career and is overjoyed to be a part of such a deeply profound project.
