Koby Polaski - Creative Producer/Actor

About Koby

Koby Polaski (24) is a creative producer for Hiking The Tide. Koby has been a part of the entertainment industry for 8 years, primarily as an actor/director. He also has had a hand in production, leading the critically acclaimed Full Fathom Productions (Emerson College) as President for 2 years, during which time he also produced, directed, and starred in Bethel Park Falls. Aside from acting, he also writes poetry, improv, and has ventured into voiceover. He feels connected to the project at a personal level, as his grieving process varies due to his mental disability.
